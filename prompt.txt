UNIQUE INNOVATION
==================================================

Dynamic Donor Circles

Each patient gets a dedicated AI-generated donor circle.

Patient
↓
8 Dedicated Donors

The system continuously evaluates donor performance.

If a donor becomes:

- inactive
- moved away
- low participation
- repeatedly unresponsive

AI recommends replacing them.

This is called:

AI Circle Optimization

One donor can belong to only one donor circle.
